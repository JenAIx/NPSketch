🔍 How It Works

NPSketch scores hand-drawn neuropsychological figures with trained CNN models. Draw or upload a figure and a model predicts its Total_Score — or, with the component model, the full 20-element × Presence/Accuracy/Position breakdown.

✏️

Draw or Upload

Sketch a figure on the canvas or upload an image; it is normalized to the training format

🧩

Component Scoring

The component model predicts 60 sub-labels (20 elements × Presence/Accuracy/Position)

🧠

Train Your Own

Train regression, classification, or component models on the unified data base

🧪

Run Tests

Batch-evaluate a model over your drawn test images and compare to expected scores
